## What changed

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `trustcal/configs/experiments.yaml` | 4 executable conditions, seeds `[1, 2]`, `sample_caps.main: 400`, `dataset_caps: {gpqa_main: 400, mmlu_pro: 400, hle: 200}`; all other conditions moved to a commented `appendix_conditions:` block |
| `trustcal/scripts/run_matrix.py` | `Matrix.dataset_caps` + `limit_override`; `Matrix.limit_for(arm, dataset)`; `_build_plan` applies the per-dataset cap; dry-run shows the condition key and cap |
| `trustcal/src/trustcal/test_matrix_budget.py` | 6 new tests: condition set, compute gate, per-dataset caps, `--limit` override, cap validation, pre-registration contents |
| `PRE_REGISTRATION.md` | Dated v1.0 pre-registration: six-arm target, primary/co-primary/secondary endpoints, power/MDE, analysis, exclusions, stopping rules, freeze record, H1/H2 interaction test |

The executable matrix is **B3**, **B4-matched** (`arm: B4`, `source_partitioning: true`),
**B8-no-trust** (`arm: B8`, `use_trust: false`), and **B8**. `B8-placebo` and
`B8-oracle` are not executable until WP-13 adds the RunConfig switches; the
pre-registration names them as the two remaining arms of the six-arm target and
schedules them as v1.1 before unblinding. The full former 15-condition grid is
preserved in the commented `appendix_conditions:` block, not in the matrix.

## Compute

- Gate calculation (`sample_caps.main` over every dataset):
  `4 conditions x 3 datasets x 2 seeds x 400 = 9,600 debates`;
  `9,600 x 8 min / 60 = 1,280 GPU-h <= 1,500`.
- Planned per-dataset rows: `4 x 2 x (400 + 400 + 200) = 8,000 debates`
  `= 1,067 GPU-h`.
