# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this research repository to be a place where people can discuss evidence, code, and writing without harassment or intimidation. We pledge to make participation respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role on the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not people.
- Give specific, verifiable feedback and accept correction.
- Credit contributors and cite sources.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data, credentials, or private annotations.
- Keep disagreements about the work proportional and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of results, citations, or authorship.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related communication in other channels.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through GitHub ([@Atik203](https://github.com/Atik203)). Do not open a public issue about a personal incident.

The maintainer will review the report, keep it confidential as far as practical, and may remove content, close a thread, require an apology, or block participation. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
